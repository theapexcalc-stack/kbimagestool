Official Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) Guidelines

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC, Lucknow) conducts key competitive tests including the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET), Rajasva Lekhpal, Gram Panchayat Adhikari (VDO), Junior Assistant, and forest guard recruitments. Uploading proper passport-size photos and signatures matching the exact portal parameters on upsssc.gov.in is mandatory to prevent form rejection.

Guidelines for UPSSSC Candidate Photographs

The photograph must be recent (taken within the last 6 months) against a white or neutral light grey background. Both ears and eyes must be clearly visible with a neutral facial expression. Caps, hats, masks, and dark tinted glasses are not permitted.

Special Guidelines for UPSSSC Candidate Signatures

UPSSSC requires the candidate to write their full name in Hindi directly below their English running-hand signature inside the same box, or sign clearly using a black/blue ink pen on unruled white paper. Avoid taking photos in dim lighting to prevent background shadows.

Frequently Asked Questions (UPSSSC Application Portal)

Q1: What is the maximum file size for UPSSSC PET forms?
For UPSSSC PET registration, candidate photographs must not exceed 50 KB and signature files must not exceed 30 KB in JPG/JPEG format.
Q2: Can I upload a signature written in capital block letters?
No. UPSSSC rules strictly state that signatures done entirely in block capital letters will result in the immediate cancellation of the application.
Q3: Are photos with colored backgrounds accepted?
No. The background should be clean white or plain light grey for optimal scanning and admit card generation.
