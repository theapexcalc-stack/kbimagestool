Privacy Policy

At KBImageTool, accessible from https://kbimagestool.netlify.app/, we take your privacy very seriously.

Local Image Processing

Unlike other online tools, your images are never uploaded to any remote server. All compression and resizing happens entirely inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas technology.

Cookies and Third-Party Ads

We may use third-party vendors (such as Google AdSense) who use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
