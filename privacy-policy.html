Privacy Policy

At KBImageTool (accessible from https://kbimagetool.in/), your privacy is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we handle and our strict zero-retention principles.

Client-Side Image & Document Processing (Zero Server Uploads)

Unlike standard web-based image conversion tools, KBImageTool operates using 100% in-browser, client-side processing:

  • Your photographs, candidate signatures, certificates, and marksheet scans are never transmitted or stored on any remote cloud server.
  • All compression, resizing, pixel adjustments, and PDF conversions happen locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and client-side JavaScript.
  • We do not have access to, nor do we log or inspect, your personal recruitment documents.

Log Files & Analytics

KBImageTool follows standard internet protocols for analytics. We use services such as Google Analytics to evaluate visitor flow, browser types, and demographic metrics on an aggregated basis to optimize overall website usability. These log files do not collect personally identifiable information (PII).

Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

Third-party advertising vendors (such as Google AdSense) may serve advertisements across our platform. These vendors use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other websites across the internet. Users may choose to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

If you have any questions or require further clarification regarding our Privacy Policy, please get in touch with us via our Contact Page.
