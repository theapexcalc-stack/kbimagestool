Why Compress Image to 50KB for Online Applications?

Most competitive examination boards, university admissions, and government recruitment portals enforce an upper file size limit of 50KB for applicant photographs. Portals such as SSC, State Police, UPPSC, and Railway recruitments automatically discard files exceeding this threshold to optimize database storage and speed up verification servers.

Maintaining Image Quality While Reducing File Size

Simple photo compression often causes text blurring or pixelation around the facial features. Our binary-search compression algorithm adjusts the quantization table and sampling rate incrementally. This ensures that the output stays strictly beneath 50KB while retaining maximum sharp detail and visual clarity required for biometric facial recognition.

No. If you choose the passport preset, the aspect ratio remains calibrated at 3.5 × 4.5 cm. Only unnecessary file metadata and excess byte overhead are trimmed down.
