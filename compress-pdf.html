Why Compress PDF Files for Recruitment Portals?

Scanned documents, certificates, and multi-page application receipts saved via home flatbed scanners or phone scanner apps regularly generate file sizes exceeding 2MB to 5MB. Most competitive examination portals (such as UPSC, SSC, Railway RRB, and State PSCs) enforce strict ceiling caps between 100KB and 300KB. Attempting to upload an oversized document leads to server timeout or file rejection errors.

Frequently Asked Questions (PDF Compression)

Q1: Can I compress a password-protected PDF?
No. You must remove the password protection from your PDF before compressing it, as portals require unencrypted files for automated parsing.
Q2: Will the text on multi-page marksheets remain readable?
Yes. The compression algorithm uses proportional font and bitmap rendering to ensure text remains crisp and legible across every page.
Q3: Is my PDF uploaded to any third-party cloud server?
No. All PDF rasterization and reconstruction happen entirely inside your web browser's local memory.
