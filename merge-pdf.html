Frequently Asked Questions (Merging PDFs)

Q1: Is there a page limit when merging PDFs?
No practical page limit exists for standard documents. You can merge multi-page marksheets and certificates seamlessly.
Q2: Can I merge password-protected PDFs?
No. You must remove password encryption from your files before merging, as recruitment portals do not accept encrypted files.
Q3: Are my uploaded certificates stored on your server?
No. Merging executes entirely in your local browser using client-side JavaScript libraries. No documents are uploaded or stored.
