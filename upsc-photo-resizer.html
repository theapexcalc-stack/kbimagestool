Official UPSC Online Application & OTR Standards

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) implements precise aspect ratio validation through its One-Time Registration (OTR) portal (upsconline.nic.in). Whether applying for the Civil Services Examination (IAS/IFS/IPS), National Defence Academy (NDA), Combined Defence Services (CDS), or Engineering Services (ESE), candidates must adhere strictly to the dual-constraint rules of pixel resolution and byte volume.

Square Dimension Rule (350×350 px)

Unlike state PSCs that accept rectangular 3.5×4.5 cm ratios, the UPSC upload algorithm expects equal width and height (aspect ratio 1:1). The pixel dimensions must not be smaller than 350 pixels in width and 350 pixels in height, nor should they exceed 1000 pixels. Our dedicated UPSC resizer guarantees a clean 350×350 pixel canvas with crisp white padding if required.

New Rule: Candidate Name & Date on Photo

In recent notices, UPSC states that the photograph uploaded must not be older than 10 days from the start of the online application process. Additionally, the candidate's name and the date on which the photograph was taken must be clearly inscribed at the bottom of the photograph.
