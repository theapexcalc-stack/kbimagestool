About KBImageTool

Welcome to KBImageTool (https://kbimagetool.in/), a dedicated web-based document processing utility created to simplify digital image and PDF formatting for competitive examinations across India.

Our Mission

Every year, millions of candidates apply for state and national government recruitments—including SSC, UPSC, Banking (IBPS & SBI), Railways (RRB), State PSCs, and Defense forces. Each recruitment portal strictly enforces tight file bounds, mandatory pixel resolutions, and specific aspect ratios. Our mission is to provide an instant, reliable, and completely free platform where applicants can prepare their photographs, signatures, and certificates accurately from any smartphone or PC.

100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture

User security and confidentiality are core foundations of KBImageTool:

  • No Server Uploads: Unlike traditional image compressors, your documents are never uploaded to remote servers.
  • Browser-Based Execution: All resizing, proportional cropping, and PDF compilation run entirely on your local machine using modern HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript engines.
  • Offline Functionality: Once the tool is loaded in your browser, you can safely resize and download images even without an active internet connection.

Dedicated Exam Presets

We actively track official notification updates to calibrate standard configurations for major recruiting agencies:

  • Staff Selection Commission (SSC): Strict 20KB–50KB photo and 10KB–20KB signature bounds.
  • UPSC OTR: High-resolution dimensional adjustments (minimum 350 × 350 px).
  • Banking Portals (IBPS/SBI): Integrated presets for left thumb impressions and handwritten declarations.
  • State Portals: Dimension adjustments for Rajasthan (RPSC/RSMSSB SSO OTR) and Uttar Pradesh (UPPSC/UPSSSC).
