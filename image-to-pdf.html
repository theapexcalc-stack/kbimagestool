Why Convert Images to PDF for Government Job Applications?

Central and state recruitment platforms (including UPSC OTR, Railway RRB, SSC, and State PSCs) mandate that educational qualification proofs, identity cards (Aadhaar, Voter ID), caste certificates, and experience records be submitted as PDF files. Uploading raw image formats like JPEG or PNG for certificates often triggers validation errors.

Frequently Asked Questions (Image to PDF Conversion)

Q1: Can I convert my phone photograph of a certificate into an official PDF?
Yes. Ensure the photo is taken in good lighting and cropped cleanly before uploading. The tool will format it into a standardized PDF.
Q2: Why does the UPSC portal reject my PDF file?
UPSC requires the PDF to be between 20 KB and 300 KB and free of password protection. This tool generates clean, unencrypted PDFs calibrated to that range.
Q3: Are my uploaded personal certificates secure?
Yes. PDF creation runs client-side inside your browser via local JavaScript. No files are transferred to remote servers.
