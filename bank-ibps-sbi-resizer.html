Official IBPS & SBI Online Upload Criteria

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) and State Bank of India (SBI) conduct annual recruitment drives for Probationary Officers (PO), Clerks (Junior Associates), and Specialist Officers (SO). Every banking application requires four digital uploads: a candidate photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and a handwritten declaration.

Rules for Banking Candidate Photograph & Signature

The candidate photograph must be recent and captured against a light-coloured or white background. Red-eye reflections, caps, dark glasses, or shadows across the face are impermissible. Signatures must strictly use black ballpoint ink. Signatures executed in CAPITAL letters are rejected, leading to application cancellation.

Rules for Thumb Impression & Handwritten Declaration

The left thumb impression must be stamped cleanly with blue or black stamp pad ink on plain white paper without smearing. The handwritten declaration must be penned exclusively in English in the candidate's normal cursive handwriting. Declarations written in block capital letters or by another individual are deemed invalid by IBPS scrutiny panels.

Frequently Asked Questions (IBPS & SBI Uploads)

Q1: Can the handwritten declaration be written in Hindi for IBPS exams?
No. Official IBPS and SBI notifications explicitly mandate that the handwritten declaration must be written in English only. Declarations submitted in Hindi or other regional languages are disqualified.
Q2: Why does the IBPS portal reject signatures signed in blue ink?
IBPS verification software uses optical scanners that require clear contrast. Signatures must be executed strictly with a black ballpoint pen on white paper.
Q3: What if my left thumb is injured or unavailable?
Under banking recruitment rules, if a candidate does not have a left thumb, they may use their right thumb impression and notify the testing authority accordingly during document verification.
